Design styles `n-morph` (`neumorph`) and `pixelate` (`pixel`), completing the six v1.0 styles (specs/004 S2b). New
token `font.family.pixel` (monospace by default) and an opt-in pixel face, `@newbrush/fonts/pixelify-sans.css`
(Pixelify Sans, OFL), which `fonts.css` does not include.
