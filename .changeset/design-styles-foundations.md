Design styles foundations (specs/004 S0): new `nb.styles` cascade layer between components and utilities; style
markers for `minimal`, `g-morph`, `n-morph`, `neon`, `cyber` and `pixelate` (class, `nb-` class, alias or
`data-nb-style`) with `.style-default` to reset a subtree; `Style` schema, `manifest.styles` and config `styles`,
`customStyles` and `prefix.styles`; `-base` aliases for surface, text, border and focus tokens.
