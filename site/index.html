newBrush

Accessible components, on-demand utilities, token-driven themes, and design styles that re-skin everything with one class. All of it is plain, modern CSS.
Get startedBrowse components

Components

Buttons, forms, tabs, modals and layout primitives on semantic HTML, each with documented anatomy, keyboard support and accessibility requirements. Browse components.

Utilities

Short, composable classes generated on demand from your markup, with variants for states, breakpoints, containers, themes and styles. See the utility reference.

Tokens and themes

Every value is a --nb-* custom property. Light, dark and high-contrast themes follow the system, or you can scope one to any element. Explore tokens.

Design styles

Put class="neon" on <body> and the whole site turns neon. Other styles: glass, neumorphic, cyber, pixel and minimal. Meet the styles.
